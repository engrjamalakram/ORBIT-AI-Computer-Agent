ORBIT - SURVIVING A POWER CUT WHILE YOU ARE OUT
==============================================

What is now automatic
---------------------
1. Starts when you log in (Task Scheduler, no delay - faster than the old
   Startup folder, which Windows deliberately delayed).
2. A watchdog checks every 3 minutes. If she is not running - crashed,
   killed, or never started - it starts her. Costs nothing when she is fine.
3. If the internet is down she does NOT give up. She waits, checking every
   5 seconds, and connects the instant the line comes back.
4. When she reconnects after being away a while, she messages you on Discord:
   "ORBIT is back online (was offline about 2h 14m)".

So: router reboots, Wi-Fi drops, she crashes -> she comes back on her own.


The one thing that still needs YOU (one-time)
---------------------------------------------
A power cut is different from a network drop, because the laptop may switch
off completely. When it powers back on, Windows stops at the LOGIN SCREEN.

Until someone logs in, there is no desktop session - so nothing can run, and
screenshots are impossible (there is literally no screen to capture).

To make recovery fully hands-free you need automatic login:

  1. Press Win+R, type:  netplwiz   and press Enter
  2. Untick "Users must enter a user name and password to use this computer"
  3. Click Apply, type your Windows password twice, OK
  4. Restart once to confirm it logs straight to the desktop

If you do not see that tickbox (common on Windows 10/11 with a Microsoft
account), enable it first:
  - Win+R -> regedit
  - Go to: HKLM\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Windows NT\CurrentVersion\PasswordLess\Device
  - Set  DevicePasswordLessBuildVersion  to  0
  - Reopen netplwiz and the tickbox appears

SECURITY NOTE: auto-login means anyone who physically opens the laptop lid
gets straight into your desktop. Only do this if the laptop stays somewhere
you trust.


Two more settings worth doing
-----------------------------
- Stop it sleeping (a sleeping laptop is an offline laptop):
    Settings > System > Power & sleep
      Screen: whatever you like
      Sleep : NEVER (at least while plugged in)
    Settings > System > Power & sleep > Additional power settings
      > Choose what closing the lid does > When plugged in: Do nothing

- Turn back on by itself after the power returns:
    Most laptops will NOT power on by themselves - the battery keeps them
    alive during a short cut, and after a long cut they stay off.
    Check your BIOS for an option like "Restore on AC Power Loss" or
    "AC Recovery" and set it to "Power On" if your model has it.
    (Many laptops do not. If yours does not, a short cut is survivable on
    battery, but a long one will need you to press the power button.)


Checking it works
-----------------
  Task Scheduler (Win+R -> taskschd.msc) -> look for "ORBIT" and "ORBIT Watchdog"

  Force a test:  open Task Manager, kill python.exe,
                 wait 3 minutes -> she should come back by herself
                 and post "back online" in Discord.

  To remove:     REMOVE_AUTOSTART.bat
